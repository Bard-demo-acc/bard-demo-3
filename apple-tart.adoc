= Apple Tart
:toc:

== At a Glance
- Yield: Makes one 23 cm tart
- Prep Time: ~30 minutes active (pastry) + time to slice apples
- Chill Time: 30 minutes
- Bake Time: 40 minutes
- Oven Temperature: 190°C
- Note: Times are approximate — see Method for sequencing.

== Ingredients
=== For the Pastry
* 250 g plain flour
* 125 g cold butter
* 50 g caster sugar
* 1 egg
* 1–2 tablespoons cold water

=== For the Filling
* 5 medium cooking apples (or eating apples — see Notes)
* 40 g caster sugar
* 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
* 1 tablespoon lemon juice
* 15 g butter

=== For Finishing (Optional)
* Extra caster sugar for sprinkling
* Leftover pastry for decorations

== Equipment
=== Required
* 23 cm tart tin
* Mixing bowl
* Rolling pin
* Knife
* Apple peeler or vegetable peeler
* Baking paper
* Wire rack
* Pastry brush

=== Optional
* Food processor (for pastry)
* Pastry cutter or bench scraper

== Notes & Preparation Tips
* Keep butter cold until you cut it into the flour.
* Read the entire recipe before starting.
* Recommended workflow: make pastry → chill pastry → prepare apples while pastry chills → prepare tin → assemble → bake.
* Apple types: cooking apples give a softer, more acidic filling; eating apples give a sweeter result.

== Method
. Make the Pastry (about 10–15 minutes active)
** Put the flour and sugar into a mixing bowl.
** Cut the cold butter into small pieces and rub it into the flour with your fingertips until the mixture resembles breadcrumbs.
** Beat the egg and add it to the mixture; mix until the pastry starts to come together.
** Add 1–2 tablespoons cold water only if necessary (avoid adding too much).
** Wrap the pastry and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.
** Tip: If the pastry is difficult to roll after chilling, leave it at room temperature for a few minutes.

. Prepare Apples While Pastry Chills (about 10–15 minutes)
** Peel and core the apples; slice them thinly and try to keep slices uniform for even cooking.
** Put the apple slices into a bowl and add the sugar, cinnamon and lemon juice; mix gently to coat.
** Note: Adjust sugar depending on apple sweetness.

. Prepare Tart Tin (toward end of chilling)
** Lightly grease the tin with butter.
** Optionally place a circle of baking paper in the base to aid removal.

. Roll Out and Fit Pastry (assembly)
** Roll the chilled pastry on a lightly floured surface into a circle slightly larger than the tart tin.
** Carefully place the pastry into the tin, press it into the base and sides, and trim any overhang.
** Prick the base several times with a fork.
** If using leftover pastry, cut shapes for decorations and reserve.

. Assemble Filling
** Arrange the prepared apple slices in an even layer over the pastry base.
** Dot the apples with the 15 g butter.
** Add pastry decorations if desired.

. Bake
** Preheat the oven to 190°C before baking.
** Place the tart on the middle shelf and bake for about 40 minutes, until the pastry is golden and the apples are soft.
** If the pastry browns too quickly, loosely cover with aluminium foil for the remainder of the baking time.
** If the pastry is golden but the apples are still firm, continue baking covered until the apples are cooked through.

. Rest and Finish
** Remove the tart from the oven and leave it in the tin for about 15 minutes to settle.
** Serve warm or at room temperature with cream, custard or vanilla ice cream.
** If not serving soon, transfer to a wire rack to cool completely before refrigerating.

== Finishing, Serving, and Storage
* Serving: Warm or room temperature; pair with cream, custard or ice cream.
* Storage: Keep at room temperature on the day it is made; cover leftovers and refrigerate if storing more than a day.
* Reheat: Bring to room temperature before serving if refrigerated.

== Troubleshooting & Tips
* Pastry Cracks When Rolling: Let it rest a few minutes to soften, then continue rolling.
* Pastry Shrinks During Baking: Often caused by stretching the pastry when lining the tin — chill pastry before baking and avoid stretching.
* Pastry Becomes Difficult to Handle: Return it to the refrigerator for a few minutes to firm up.
* Browning Too Fast: Cover with foil.
* Timing Tip: Prepare apples while pastry chills to use downtime effectively.

== Variations & Notes
* Apple Varieties: Suggest a mix of tart and sweet apples for balanced flavor.
* Make-Ahead: Pastry can be made and chilled earlier; apples can be prepared just before assembly.
